# Changelog

All notable changes to Happy Bird. The newest version is at the top.
Downloads for every version are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/25k2rrgy62-oss/happy-bird/releases).

## 1.14.0-alpha (2026-11-13)

This is a test version. It has its own download page and does not replace the finished 1.13.0 release.

### Added
- **3 new game modes**, picked from a new **Mode** button on the home screen that opens a list with a description and your best score for each:
  - **Wobble:** the gaps slide up and down.
  - **Hardcore:** tighter gaps and faster pipes, but every coin counts double.
  - **Rush:** 60 seconds on the clock. A crash costs 5 seconds instead of ending the run. No medals.
- **Extras**, a second thing to wear together with a hat: Scarf (80), Bow tie (120), Hero cape (200), Sneakers (250), Jetpack (400), Fancy tail (600), Angel wings (1,000, needs Gold) and Royal cape (1,800, needs Diamond). Choose one in Settings → Extra.
- **Pets** that float along behind your bird: Baby chick (150), Balloon (250), Bee (350), Butterfly (500), Tiny cloud (700), Little ghost (900), UFO (1,400, needs Platinum) and Lucky star (2,000, needs Diamond). Choose one in Settings → Pet.
- **3 new abilities** in Shop → Deals, each with 3 levels like the Coin Magnet:
  - **Lucky Coins:** more sparkly coins (24%, 30%, 36% instead of 18%).
  - **Cheap Revive:** revives cost 20%, 35%, 50% less.
  - **Starter Shield:** every run starts with a shield that blocks one crash for the first 10, 15, 20 seconds. Not in Versus.
- **Player levels:** every pipe is 1 XP (half in Easy mode). Each new level pays coins, and your level shows on the home screen, the game-over card and in Stats.
- **Expert bot** in Versus → Play a bot. It crashes far less often than the Hard bot.
- **Bots have their own random look** every match: either a plain bird colour or a special bird, sometimes with a hat and sometimes with an extra. Holiday-only items are never used. Before, the bot looked exactly like your own bird.
- **Perfect passes:** fly through the middle of a gap for a bonus coin and a "PERFECT" pop-up. Do it pipe after pipe to build a combo ("PERFECT ×5"); the sound climbs higher as the streak grows. New daily mission: "Fly N perfect passes". Stats track your perfect passes and best streak.
- **Top runs:** Stats shows your 5 best runs (with dates) for the mode you have selected.
- **Online rivals look like themselves:** in Versus you now see the other player's real bird, hat and extra instead of a copy of your own.
- Small details: "NEW BEST!" when you beat your best mid-run (and the score turns gold), confetti every 10 points, "GO!" when a Versus countdown ends, and the coin counter bounces when you earn coins.
- **Coins for beating a bot**, more for tougher bots: Easy 5, Normal 15, Hard 40, Expert 100. The Versus panel lists the rewards.

### Changed
- The Shop has two more tabs, Extras and Pets.
- The game-over card shows coins and XP on one line, hides pop-up messages, and stays below the top buttons on small screens.
- Versus still uses Classic rules whatever mode is selected, so alpha players can be matched with 1.13.0 players.

### Fixed
- The rival bird in Versus (bots and online players) now flaps its wings and tilts as it flies. Before, it was a single frozen picture that only slid up and down.

## 1.13.0 (2026-11-13)

### Added
- **Pause:** a Pause button in the top-left while you fly (or press P). The game also pauses by itself when you switch apps or a call comes in. Tap to keep flying. Matches against real players can't be paused.
- **Android back button:** closes the open panel, pauses a run, leaves the game-over screen or cancels a match search. On the home screen it minimises the app instead of quitting.
- **Share your score:** a SHARE button on the game-over screen opens the phone's share sheet with your score, medal and a download link.

## 1.12.0 (2026-11-07)

### Changed
- **The Shop has tabs:** Deals, Maps, Colors, Birds, Hats and Trails. You see one section at a time instead of one long list, and the tab bar stays at the top while you scroll.
- The Shop opens on the tab you used last.
- The Deals tab gets a red dot while a limited-time holiday event is on.

## 1.11.0 (2026-11-07)

### Added
- **7 new maps:** Desert (200), Jungle with fireflies (300), Arctic with snow (400), Volcano with rising embers (600), City Night with rain (800), Neon (1,200) and Royal Gold (2,500). The Shop shows a small preview colour for every map.
- **7 new special birds:** Penguin (300), Zombie (400), Ice (500), Phoenix (800), Galaxy (1,200), Diamond (1,800) and Legend (2,500).
- **8 new hats:** Chef hat (150), Flower crown (200), Cowboy hat (250), Devil horns (300), Viking helmet (400), Pirate hat (500), Halo (1,000) and Diamond crown (2,000).
- **6 new bird colours:** Mint, Ocean, Rose, Lime, Midnight and Coal (60–150).
- **Trails**, a new kind of item: particles that follow your bird. Sparkles (200), Bubbles (300), Hearts (400), Leaves (500), Fire (800), Stardust (1,200) and Rainbow (2,000). Choose one in Settings → Trail.
- **Medal locks for the top items:** the most expensive items also need a medal before you can buy them (Phoenix needs Gold; Galaxy, Neon, Halo and Stardust need Platinum; Diamond bird, Diamond crown and Rainbow trail need Diamond; Legend bird and Royal Gold need Legend). A better medal also counts.

## 1.10.0 (2026-11-07)

### Changed
- **Faster gameplay:** pipes move about 16% faster at the start and speed up a bit more as your score grows.
- **Bigger gaps to balance it:** the opening between pipes starts at 90 instead of 80 and never gets smaller than 62 (was 54). Easy mode gaps grew by the same amount.
- Versus only matches players on this version or newer, so both players always get the same pipes.

## 1.9.0 (2026-11-07)

### Changed
- **Chaos mode balance:** a roll timer at the top of the screen now gives you a random power-up or debuff every 5 seconds, so dodging every orb is no longer a safe strategy. It counts down ("ROLL IN 3"), then spins through the effects like a slot machine before landing.
- Effects from the roll don't count toward stats or the "Catch power-ups" mission. Only orbs you catch yourself do.

## 1.8.0 (2026-11-07)

### Added
- **Players online:** the home screen and the Versus panel show how many players are online.
- **Play while you wait:** after you tap Find Match you can fly practice runs. A bar at the bottom shows the search time and has only a Cancel button. Practice runs don't give coins, stats or medals.
- New effects: a puff of air when you flap, feathers when you crash, a shadow under the bird and soft shadows beside the pipes.

### Improved
- Drawing is about 3.7× faster (less battery use and smoother on older phones), with the same look.

## 1.7.0 (2026-11-06)

### Added
- **Coin Magnet** ability in the Shop: 3 levels that pull floating coins toward you in every mode.
- **Versus bots:** Easy, Normal and Hard bots that race you on the same pipes. Works offline.
- **Easy mode:** wider gaps, slower pipes and softer gravity, with its own best score (no medals).

## 1.6.0 (2026-11-04)

### Added
- **Versus matchmaking:** Find Match pairs you with another player who is searching.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-04)

### Changed
- Missions are harder and pay more (30–75 coins).
- Medals are farther apart, and there are two new ones: Bronze 15, Silver 30, Gold 50, Platinum 75, **Diamond 100**, **Legend 150**.

### Added
- Version number on the start screen and in the app info.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-04)

### Added
- **5 daily missions** that change every day.
- **Online Versus:** race a friend with a 4-letter room code, with the same pipes and a ghost bird.

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-04)

### Changed
- The Android and iPhone apps are built from the same game file by one build.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-04)

### Changed
- Chaos mode orbs all look the same: you only find out if it's a power-up or a debuff when you catch it.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-04)

### Added
- Missions, Stats, a daily reward with streaks, medals, vibration, and **Chaos mode** with power-ups, debuffs and mystery boxes.

### Fixed
